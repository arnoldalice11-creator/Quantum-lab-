📚 Lesson 1: Wave-Particle Duality

In our world, a light switch is either ON or OFF. But in quantum physics, light and electrons can behave like a wave (like ripples on water) AND a particle (like tiny marbles) at the exact same time!

🧮 Lesson: Quantum Probability

Instead of knowing exactly where an electron is, quantum math uses probability waves ($\Psi$). The higher the wave crest, the higher the chance of finding the electron there!
