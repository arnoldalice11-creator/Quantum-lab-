🧮 Quantum Maths & Wave Equations

In quantum mechanics, particles are described by the Schrödinger Wave Equation:

iℏ (∂/∂t)Ψ(x,t) = ĤΨ(x,t)

Key Variables:

  • Ψ (Psi): The wave function (probability amplitude).
  • ℏ (h-bar): Reduced Planck's constant.
  • Ĥ: The Hamiltonian operator (total energy).
